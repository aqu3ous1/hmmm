HOW THE POD WORKS

Controls

  • W A S DMove
  • MouseLook
  • Left clickFire / swing
  • ShiftSprint
  • SpaceJump
  • Ctrl / CDodge roll (brief invulnerability)
  • RReload
  • QSwap weapon
  • 1 / 2Select slot
  • EInteract — chests, panels, the lift
  • FDrop the active weapon
  • TabLoadout & synergy detail
  • MMute audio
  • EscPause

Rules of the climb

  • Two weapons, no requests. Every chest rolls at random. Two chests per floor and two hands — something always gets left behind.
  • Synergies are found, not chosen. Certain pairs power each other up. Certain pairs refuse to cooperate and both get worse. Mix and match.
  • Clear the objective, then the boss. Each floor locks its final door until the objective is done.
  • Shards buy nothing you need and everything you want. Spend them at Vend-o-Trons for ammo, health, and re-rolls.
  • Dying costs you the floor, not the run. The Pod is patient. That is the problem.
